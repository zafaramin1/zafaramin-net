Singer · Guitarist · Songwriter · Author

Zafar Amin

I have been writing songs since I was a little boy. I sang and played guitar in Son of the Soil and The SandGuineas, two punk rock bands in Southern California, and wrote every song we played.

Our first album, Born Free (2005), came out under Son of the Soil. In 2006 we played three nights at the World Performing Arts Festival in Lahore, Pakistan, with Afzaal and Justin from the Los Angeles band Crisis.

Today I write stories too. My first book is Gogo Guardians, an illustrated adventure and riddle game for kids.
