01  About

Businesses work hard to win a customer, then lose touch the moment they walk out the door.

I founded IDDAS Technologies to close that gap. Our answer is CandleMe, a digital candle that lives on a customer's phone and in Apple or Google Wallet, so the business stays one tap away.

Underneath it sits the IDDAS Protocol, which records real-world activation and attribution in a way anyone can verify.

The same platform reaches students and teachers through Student Launch Path, bringing free career resources to campuses and classrooms.

Today it serves car washes, auto dealerships, salons and beauty, education, hospitality and industrial laundry.
