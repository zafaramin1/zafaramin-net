01  About

Businesses work hard to win a customer, then lose touch the moment they walk out the door.

I founded IDDAS Technologies to close that gap. Our answer is CandleMe, a digital candle that lives on a customer's phone and in Apple or Google Wallet, so the business stays one tap away.

Underneath it sits the IDDAS Protocol, which records real-world activation and attribution in a way anyone can verify.

The same platform reaches students and teachers through Student Launch Path, bringing free career resources to campuses and classrooms.

Today it serves car washes, auto dealerships, salons and beauty, education, hospitality and industrial laundry.

Education: teach with us

Teach with TutorABC

Touch to flip

I am part of TutorABC’s teacher recruitment team. These English teaching roles are open now, and we encourage you to apply. TutorABC trains its teachers on the Cambridge English Teaching Framework.

The role

  • Remote contract, with a flexible schedule you set yourself.
  • Teach children, university students, working professionals, or adults who want to speak more fluently.
  • All teaching materials are provided before each class.

What you need

  • A bachelor’s degree or higher.
  • A recognized English teaching certificate, such as TESOL, TEFL, TESL or CELTA.
  • At least 1 year of ESL teaching experience in the United States, or 2 years in other countries.

Nice to have

  • A second language at B1 level or higher.
  • Advanced ESL qualifications, such as CELTA, TKT, DipTESOL, PGCE or MEd.
  • Experience teaching international learners.

Where we are hiring now

Each opening is for teachers who live in that country. It is about where you live, not your nationality. Please apply through the opening for the country you live in. Applications sent to another country’s opening can take longer to reach the right team, or may not be considered there.
