About

Founder, builder, storyteller.

I founded IDDAS Technologies to solve a simple problem: businesses work hard to win a customer, then lose touch the moment that customer walks out the door.

Our answer is CandleMe, a digital candle that lives on a customer's phone and in Apple or Google Wallet, and the IDDAS Protocol underneath it, which records real-world activation and attribution in a way anyone can verify.

Through Student Launch Path, the same platform helps students and teachers. And as an author, I wrote Gogo Guardians, an illustrated adventure and riddle game for kids.
